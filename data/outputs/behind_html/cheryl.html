Meaning & History

Expand Links
Elaboration of Cherie, perhaps influenced by Beryl [1][2]. This name was very rare before the 20th century. It seems to have been popularized in America by the actress Cheryl Walker (1918-1971), who had a prominent role in the 1943 movie Stage Door Canteen. After peaking in the 1950s the name has subsequently faded from the popularity charts.
