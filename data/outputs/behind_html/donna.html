Meaning & History

Expand Links
From Italian donna meaning "lady". It is also used as a feminine form of Donald or Don [1]. It grew in popularity starting around the 1920s, reaching a peak in the American rankings in 1959. The musician Ritchie Valens released a song by this name in 1958.
