Meaning & History

Means "sorrows", taken from the Spanish title of the Virgin Mary Nuestra Señora de los Dolores, meaning "Our Lady of Sorrows" [1]. It has been used in the English-speaking world since the 19th century, becoming especially popular in America during the 1920s and 30s.
