Meaning & History

Expand Links
Perhaps intended to be a feminine form of Samuel, using the name suffix antha (possibly inspired by Greek ἄνθος (anthos) meaning "flower") [1]. It originated in America in the 18th century but was fairly uncommon until 1964, when it was popularized by the main character on the television show Bewitched [2].
