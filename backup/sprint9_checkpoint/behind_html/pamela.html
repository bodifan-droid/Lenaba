Meaning & History

This name was invented in the late 16th century by the poet Philip Sidney for use in his romance Arcadia (1593) [1]. He possibly intended it to mean "all sweetness" from Greek πᾶν (pan) meaning "all" and μέλι (meli) meaning "honey" [2][3]. It was later employed by author Samuel Richardson for the heroine in his novel Pamela, or Virtue Rewarded (1740), after which time it became used as a given name [1]. It did not become popular until the 20th century.
