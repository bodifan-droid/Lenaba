Meaning & History

From Aramaic מַרְתָּא (marta) meaning "the lady, the mistress" [1][2], feminine form of מָרֵא (mare) meaning "master" [3]. In the New Testament this is the name of the sister of Lazarus and Mary of Bethany (who is sometimes identified with Mary Magdalene). She was a witness to Jesus restoring her dead brother to life.

The name was not used in England until after the Protestant Reformation. A notable bearer was Martha Washington (1731-1802), the wife of the first American president George Washington. It is also borne by the media personality Martha Stewart (1941-).
