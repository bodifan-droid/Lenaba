Meaning & History

From an English surname that was originally derived from a place name meaning "bright clearing", from Old English scir "bright" and leah "woodland, clearing" [1][2][3]. This is the name of a main character in Charlotte Brontë's semi-autobiographical novel Shirley (1849). Though the name was already popular in the United States, the child actress Shirley Temple (1928-2014) gave it a further boost. By 1935 it was the second most common name for girls.
